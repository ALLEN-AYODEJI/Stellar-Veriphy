# ADR-0008: AI-generated content labeling

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Deciders:** Core maintainers

## Context

AI-generated and AI-assisted media is increasingly difficult to distinguish from
human-created content.  Emerging regulations (EU AI Act, US AI disclosure
frameworks, C2PA standard) require platforms that certify digital media to
communicate the role of AI in content creation clearly and without overstating
certainty.

StellarVeriphy certifies content provenance on-chain.  Without a labeling
mechanism, a provenance certificate gives no signal about whether the certified
content was generated by a model — a significant gap for journalism, legal, and
scientific use cases.

Closes #673.

## Options considered

1. **Block AI-generated content entirely** — simplest policy, but prevents
   legitimate use cases (AI artwork, AI-assisted research data, etc.) and does
   not serve the disclosure goal.

2. **Require a mandatory binary "is AI-generated" flag on every upload** —
   forces a binary decision where confidence is often partial.  Overstates
   certainty and adds friction for human-created content.

3. **Optional, non-destructive label with explicit confidence and source** —
   labels annotate provenance records without altering or blocking them.
   The label carries a `confidence` value and a `detectionSource` so
   consumers always know how reliable the classification is.  Chosen.

## Decision

1. Add `packages/shared/ai-labeling/index.ts` with:
   - `AiLabel` — confidence + generation type + detection source.
   - `AiLabelRecord` — ordered label history with a resolved display label.
   - `resolveDisplayLabel()` — highest-trust source, then most recent, wins.
   - `describeLabel()` — human-readable description that never overstates certainty.
   - `creatorDeclaredLabel()` and `detectorLabel()` — factory helpers.

2. Add `POST /api/uploads/:id/ai-label` and `GET /api/uploads/:id/ai-label`
   endpoints to attach and retrieve labels.

3. Labels feed the compliance policy engine (ADR-0009) — if a label of
   `fully_generated` is present, the policy engine can apply restrictions
   appropriate to the asset class and jurisdiction.

## Consequences

- Labels are additive and non-destructive: existing certificates are not
  affected and no labels are required.
- The confidence-first design avoids overstating certainty for heuristic or
  model-based detectors.  Any label with `confidence < 0.7` is displayed with
  a disclaimer.
- The trust-ordering on `detectionSource` ensures that a TEE-attested or
  platform-reviewed label supersedes a heuristic one, making the system safe
  to extend with automated detectors without polluting high-trust use cases.
- When C2PA tooling matures, the `tee_verified` source tier is the natural
  integration point.
