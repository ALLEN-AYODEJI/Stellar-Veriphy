# Understanding Blockchain Provenance

Provenance refers to the origin and history of an object. In the digital world, blockchain technology provides a powerful way to establish and verify provenance.

## What is Provenance?

Provenance is the complete record of the ownership, custody, and location of an object or piece of information throughout its existence.

## How Blockchain Enables Provenance

### Immutability
Once data is recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be altered or deleted. This creates an unchangeable history of ownership and modifications.

### Transparency
All transactions are visible to all participants in the network, creating complete transparency in the chain of custody.

### Decentralization
No single entity controls the records, making it impossible for any party to manipulate the history.
