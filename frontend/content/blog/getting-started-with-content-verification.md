# Getting Started with Content Verification

Content verification is essential in today's digital world. This guide will help you understand the basics of using Stellar-Veriphy.

## What is Content Verification?

Content verification is the process of proving that a piece of content existed at a specific time and hasn't been modified since.

## Why Use Stellar-Veriphy?

- **Decentralized**: Uses the Stellar blockchain
- **Transparent**: Immutable record of verification
- **Secure**: Cryptographic hashing ensures integrity
- **User-Friendly**: Simple interface for everyone

## Quick Start

1. Navigate to the verification page
2. Upload or paste your content
3. Click "Verify"
4. Share your verification proof

## Next Steps

After verifying your content, you can:
- Export the verification certificate
- Share the proof with others
- Track the verification history
- Build a portfolio of verified content
